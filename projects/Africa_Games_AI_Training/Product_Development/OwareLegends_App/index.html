How to Play

The 1,000-year-old strategy game of Africa

  1. Tap one of your pits (bottom row) to scoop its seeds and sow them one-by-one, counter-clockwise.
  2. If your last seed lands in an opponent pit making it 2 or 3 seeds, you capture them — plus any unbroken chain of 2s and 3s just behind it.
  3. You may never starve your opponent: if they have no seeds, you must play a move that reaches their side.
  4. First to capture 25 seeds wins!
