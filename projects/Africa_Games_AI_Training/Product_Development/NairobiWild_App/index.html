How to play

Spot three of the same animal and you're away.

  1. Swap two animals that sit side by side.
  2. Line up three or more and the herd moves on — you score.
  3. Match 4 for a striped animal that clears a whole line. Match 5 for the 🌈 — swap it onto any animal to clear every one of them.
  4. Make an L or T shape for a stampede that clears everything around it.
  5. Reach the target before the moves run out.
