NAIROBI WILD — SOUND PACK CREDITS
=================================

Every audio file shipped in this folder MUST be listed below before the
game is published. Missing attribution on a CC-BY file is a licence
breach; unlicensed audio is a takedown and, for a paid or ad-supported
app, a legal exposure.

Format — one line per file:

  <path>  |  <source>  |  <author>  |  <licence>  |  <url>

Example (replace with real entries):

  01_LION/roar_01.ogg | Freesound | someauthor | CC0 1.0 | https://freesound.org/s/XXXXXX/
  18_SAVANNA_AMBIENCE/savanna_loop.ogg | own recording | <your name> | © <year> <studio>, all rights reserved | —

CHECKLIST BEFORE SHIPPING
  [ ] Every file in this pack appears above.
  [ ] Every CC-BY file is credited in the app's in-game credits screen.
  [ ] Every commercial-pack licence permits use in a PAID or AD-SUPPORTED app.
  [ ] No audio taken from YouTube, film, television or another game.
  [ ] Recordings you made yourself are marked as owned, with the date.
