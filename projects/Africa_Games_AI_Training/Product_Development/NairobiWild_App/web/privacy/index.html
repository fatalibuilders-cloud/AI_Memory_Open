Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

The short version: this game collects nothing about you.

There is no account, no sign-up and no analytics. Your stars, coins and settings are saved on your own phone and are never sent anywhere. We cannot see them, and neither can anyone else.

This policy covers the game Nairobi Wild — played on the web at fatalibuilders-cloud.github.io/AI_Memory_Open, installed to a phone's home screen from that address, or installed as an Android app.

What the game saves on your device

The game keeps one record in your browser's local storage, under the name nairobiWild.v1. It holds:

  • which levels you have finished and how many stars you earned
  • your coins, lives and daily streak
  • your sound and music settings
  • the nickname you type in, if you use the duel screens

This stays on your device. The game has no server to send it to. Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it permanently — we hold no copy and cannot restore it.

The Android app asks for no permissions at all — not even internet access. Once installed, the whole game is on your phone.

The one third party: our web host

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every website, visiting it means your device contacts GitHub's servers, and GitHub records technical information such as your IP address for security and abuse-prevention purposes. That happens at the hosting level, before our game runs, and we neither see nor receive it. GitHub's handling of it is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

After your first visit the game is stored on your phone and plays with no network connection at all, so it need not contact anyone again.

Challenge links

If you choose to send a friend a duel challenge, the link you send contains your nickname, your score and a number that recreates the same board. That information travels in the link itself — through whatever app you choose to send it with — and does not pass through us. Nothing is sent until you tap share, and you decide who receives it.

Backups

The Android app permits Android's standard backup, so your saved progress may be included in the backup your phone makes to your own Google account. That backup belongs to you and is governed by Google's terms; we have no access to it. You can switch it off in your phone's own backup settings.

Children

Nairobi Wild is suitable for all ages and contains nothing unsuitable for children. Because the game collects no personal information from anybody, it collects none from children either. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, of any age.

Ads and purchases

This version of the game shows no advertising and offers no purchases. If that changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the Play Store listing's Data safety section will be updated in the same release — because an advertising provider would collect data that we currently do not.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to show you, correct or delete. Everything the game knows about you is on your own device and entirely under your control: clear the site data in your browser, or uninstall the app, and it is gone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will accompany a new release of the game rather than being made quietly.
