How to play

If you can spot three of the same, you can win.

  1. Swap any two goods that sit side by side.
  2. Line up three or more and they sell — you score.
  3. Match 4 for a striped good that clears a whole line. Match 5 for the 🌈 rainbow — swap it onto any good to clear every one of them.
  4. Make an L or T shape for a bomb that clears everything around it.
  5. Hit the target before the moves run out. Goals at the top show what the stall needs.
