# Africa Games — Go-to-Market (Pan-African)

**Two titles, one funnel.** **Market Day** (match-3) is the acquisition engine — the genre with the largest proven audience. **Oware Legends** (mancala) is the retention and credibility play, cross-promoted to players who want more depth.

**Market Day positioning:** *"The market you already know, in your pocket."* Everything on the board — cowries, maize, mangoes, greens, fish, kola — is a good any generation recognizes. Creatives should show a real stall cutting to the phone.

**Oware Legends positioning:** *"The game your grandmother can beat you at."* — Africa's own strategy game, finally done justice on the phone.

---

## 1. The unfair advantage
Oware/Ayo/Awalé/Warri/Adji is already known in virtually every African country. Marketing does not need to explain the game — it needs to trigger recognition and pride. Every creative should open with the physical board (market stalls, family compounds, beach benches) cutting to the phone.

## 2. Naming & localization
- Keep **Oware Legends** as the master brand, but surface local names prominently: app-store listings localized as "Oware Legends — Ayo" (NG), "— Awalé" (FR West Africa), "— Warri" (GH), "— Adji" (TG/BJ).
- Launch languages: English, French, Swahili, Hausa, Yoruba, Twi, Portuguese, Arabic, Amharic, Zulu (strings are ~50 short lines — cheap to do well).
- All copy avoids idioms that don't translate; numbers and emoji carry meaning.

## 3. Priority markets (phased)
| Phase | Markets | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Soft launch | Ghana, Kenya | High Oware/mancala familiarity, mature mobile money, moderate UA costs, English |
| Wave 1 | Nigeria, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Tanzania | Scale + francophone proof |
| Wave 2 | Egypt, Ethiopia, South Africa, Cameroon, DRC | Scale; SA for ad eCPMs |
| Diaspora | UK, France, US | High-ARPU diaspora communities; Warri is huge in the Caribbean |

## 4. Distribution
- **Google Play** first (TWA/wrapped WebView keeps APK < 5 MB — a marketing feature in itself: advertise the tiny size).
- **Direct APK + web version** — APK sharing via Xender/Bluetooth is a real distribution channel; the web build (this repo) is shareable as a single link and works offline after first load.
- Feature-phone follow-up: KaiOS port is feasible since the game is plain HTML/JS.

## 5. Growth loops (built into product roadmap)
1. **Pass-and-play** is already in v0.1 — the phone gets handed around; every second player is an organic install prospect.
2. **Challenge links** (roadmap): send a board state via WhatsApp — "finish this game against me." WhatsApp is the continent's social graph.
3. **Tournaments** with city/country leaderboards → local pride → shares.
4. **Campus & barbershop circuits:** physical Oware is already played there; micro-sponsorships of existing offline tournaments cost almost nothing and buy authenticity.

## 6. Launch beats
- Tie Wave 1 launch to **AFCON** (continental attention + national-pride themes on sale).
- PR angle: "African classic beats imported games" + founder story; pitch TechCabal, Techpoint Africa, Disrupt Africa.
- Creator strategy: mid-tier comedy/gaming creators in Lagos, Accra, Nairobi playing elders at Oware and losing. UGC formats beat polished ads here.

## 7. KPIs
- D1/D7/D30 retention: 45% / 20% / 8% targets for soft launch (strategy games over-index on D30).
- K-factor from pass-and-play + challenge links ≥ 0.15 before paid UA scales.
- Cost caps: CPI ≤ $0.15 (GH/KE soft launch), blended ROAS ≥ 120% by D60 before Wave 1.
