# Key Decisions — Africa Games

| # | Date | Decision | Rationale | Keywords |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-07-16 | First game = **Oware (mancala)**, not an original casual concept | Pre-existing continent-wide familiarity (Ayo/Awalé/Warri/Adji) removes the hardest problem (teaching a new game); chess-class depth drives long-term retention | game-choice, oware, mancala, retention |
| 2 | 2026-07-16 | **Abapa (tournament) rules**: grand-slam forfeit, feeding obligation, 25-to-win | The variant used in competitive play; unambiguous, well-documented, fair | rules, abapa, grand-slam, feeding |
| 3 | 2026-07-16 | **HTML5-first, tiny payload, zero assets, offline** | African data costs and low-end Android are the market; tiny size is both engineering and marketing | html5, data-light, offline, low-end |
| 4 | 2026-07-16 | **Ethical monetization**: rewarded ads (opt-in), cosmetic IAP, tournament passes; NO pay-to-win or loot boxes | Long-term brand trust; gambling-adjacent mechanics are a legal risk in several jurisdictions | monetization, rewarded-ads, iap, ethics |
| 5 | 2026-07-16 | **Mobile money is first-class**: Flutterwave/Paystack (M-Pesa, MoMo, Airtel, airtime) alongside Play Billing | Card penetration is low; mobile money is how the continent pays | payments, mobile-money, mpesa, flutterwave |
| 6 | 2026-07-16 | Monetization isolated behind one `Monetization` object seam | SDK integration/testing/AB-swaps never touch game code | architecture, seam, sdk |
| 7 | 2026-07-16 | Engines as pure, tested functions shared between browser and Node | Rules bugs get caught in CI, not production; enables future server-side re-validation | engine, testing, determinism |
| 8 | **2026-08-30** | **Owner redirect: the flagship is a match-3 like Candy Crush, "addictive for both old and new generation."** Built **Market Day**; Oware Legends becomes the second title. | The owner's vision is the mass-market casual genre, not a board game. Match-3 has the largest proven audience and the clearest monetization; Oware stays as the retention/credibility title. | pivot, match-3, candy-crush, flagship, market-day |
| 9 | 2026-08-30 | **The generational bridge is the theme, not the mechanic.** Standard match-3 rules; tiles are market goods (cowries, maize, mango, greens, fish, kola). | Young players already know the mechanic; older players already know the goods. Changing the mechanic would break the first half of that; abstract jewels would break the second. | theme, generations, accessibility, differentiation |
| 10 | 2026-08-30 | **Relax Mode ships in v1** — unlimited moves, no lives, no failure, free forever. | Resolves the tension between decision #4 (no lockouts) and the genre-standard lives economy. Lives apply to the level campaign only, so no one is ever locked out of playing. | relax-mode, lives, accessibility, ethics |
| 11 | 2026-08-30 | **Lives economy adopted for the campaign** (5 max, 10-min regen, refund on win, ad/coin refill) — a deliberate softening of the original "no energy gates" stance. | Lives are the engine of match-3 monetization and session pacing; refusing them would gut the business model. Generous parameters plus Relax Mode keep it non-exploitative. | lives, energy, monetization, tradeoff |
| 12 | 2026-08-30 | Avoid **"Crush"** and **"Saga"** in any final product name. | King (Candy Crush) enforces those trademarks aggressively; a rename after launch would be expensive. | legal, trademark, naming |
| 13 | 2026-08-30 | UI plays back engine-produced **phases**; it never re-derives rules. | The animation can never disagree with the score, and cascades stay debuggable. | architecture, animation, phases |
| 14 | 2026-08-30 | Project restructured as a **two-game studio** (`Africa_Games_AI_Training`). | Folder named after one game misleads future sessions once a second, larger title exists. | structure, memory, naming |
| 15 | 2026-08-30 | **Setting is Nairobi; tiles are Kenyan wildlife, not market goods.** Renamed Market Day → **Nairobi Wild**. | Owner's call — the game comes from Nairobi. Wildlife is the single most recognisable Kenyan visual language, reads at any age, and gives six shapes that are trivially distinguishable. Levels became a journey out of Nairobi National Park to the Mara. | theme, nairobi, kenya, animals, rename |
| 16 | 2026-08-30 | **Offline solo AND online multiplayer both ship.** Five modes: Safari, Relax, pass-and-play, challenge link, live online duel. | Owner asked for both. Only one of the five needs a network, which matters where data is expensive and intermittent. | multiplayer, offline, modes |
| 17 | 2026-08-30 | **Duels are same-seed races**, not turn-based — both players get an identical board and the higher score wins. | The engine is deterministic, so a shared seed is all the "sync" a fair match needs. It makes offline (link) and online duels the same game, and keeps bandwidth to a trickle of score updates. | duel, determinism, seed |
| 18 | 2026-08-30 | **Live online is built on the artifact `room` capability, using presence only — never events.** | Presence is settable by any viewer; event topics are admin-only by default, so an events-based lobby would work for the owner and silently fail for everyone else. | online, room, presence, architecture |
| 19 | 2026-08-30 | **Client-reported scores are accepted for friendly duels; ranked play is deferred** until an authoritative server exists. | Honest scope: friends duelling do not need anti-cheat, and shipping a fake "ranked" mode on trustable-client scores would be worse than not shipping one. The deterministic engine lets a future server verify by replaying moves. | anti-cheat, ranked, integrity |
| 20 | 2026-08-30 | **All music and sound is synthesised at runtime — no audio files, ever.** A Benga-flavoured generative groove. | A licensed music bed costs megabytes on a prepaid data bundle and needs clearing per market. Synthesis keeps the install tiny and the rights clean, and Benga is Nairobi's own guitar style rather than generic "African drums". | music, benga, assets, data-light, licensing |
| 21 | 2026-08-30 | **Duels never cost a life.** | Multiplayer is the growth loop; taxing it with the energy economy would suppress the exact behaviour we want. | lives, multiplayer, economy |
