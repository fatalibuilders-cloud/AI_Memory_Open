# Key Decisions — 2026-07-16 1007

**Session Goal:** Record the owner's redefinition of the product: public app, data-in → results-out, $30 lifetime access.

## Decisions Made

### Decision 1: The app is a PUBLIC Fatalibuilders product, not an internal tool (RESHAPES the AI-drafted vision)
- **Context:** The original AI-drafted vision assumed an internal field/office management tool. The owner clarified: "it's a Fatalibuilders product but anyone can use as long as they log in."
- **Decision:** Public product under the Fatalibuilders brand; user accounts and login required for access.
- **Rationale:** Owner directive. (The owner noted they had stated this earlier; recording it now as the authoritative direction.)
- **Impact:** Vision rewritten; target audience broadened to contractors/builders/site engineers/homeowners; Marketing and Legal dependencies become essential (GTM, terms of service, privacy, refunds); architecture gains signup/login and access gating.

### Decision 2: Core interaction — user inserts data, app gives out results
- **Context:** Owner: "the app should give an option to the end user to insert certain data for it to give out results."
- **Decision:** The heart of the product is a data-in → results-out tool. Exact inputs/outputs NOT yet defined.
- **Rationale:** Owner directive.
- **Impact:** New Epic 2 (Core Tool). **Blocking open question:** what data in, what results out (material quantities? cost estimate? labor/time?). Also open: whether earlier management features (job tracking, scheduling, site logs) stay in scope (parked as Epic 5).

### Decision 3: Monetization — one-time $30 for lifetime access
- **Context:** Owner: "make it to monetary terms like life time access for 30$."
- **Options Considered:** Owner directive; advisor lens applied (Growth-n-Revenue advisor, `Executive/agents/Growth-n-Revenue-advisor.md`): lifetime pricing fixes LTV at $30 while costs are ongoing; no expansion revenue path.
- **Decision:** $30 lifetime access stands for release 1. Advisor-informed guardrails recorded: keep per-user infrastructure cost minimal; treat lifetime as a launch offer that can become one tier among others; leave room for a future pro tier; validate willingness-to-pay with first users.
- **Rationale:** Owner directive; simple to communicate and low-friction for the target market. Guardrails preserve future flexibility without contradicting the owner.
- **Impact:** Payments become part of Epic 1 (payment provider needed — Stripe/Paddle/Lemon Squeezy; merchant-of-record options flagged for a first-time seller). API-Keys-Guide.md updated: a payment provider account is now needed at build/launch of the checkout step. Legal: refund policy and digital sales tax noted.
