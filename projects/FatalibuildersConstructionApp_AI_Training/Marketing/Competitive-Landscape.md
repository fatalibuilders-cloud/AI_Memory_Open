# Competitive Landscape — is there anything else like this?

**For:** Eng Ali Ahmed · **Date:** 2026-08-05 · **Method:** web background check

**Short answer:** No single product is the *exact* combination Fatalibuilders is
(dimensions-in → engineering-grade quantities + costed BOQ + labour plan, design-code
aware, one-time $30, M-Pesa, mobile-first, self-serve). **But every individual
piece has competitors, and the market is more contested than the earlier Provecta
reports implied.** This is a real, occupied market — differentiation and execution
matter more than "we're the only one."

---

## 1. Kenya self-build cost tools — the closest direct competitors ⚠️

These already occupy the "estimate my Kenyan build" space:
- **BuildCostKenya** (Google Play) — material quantity calculator, labour estimator,
  equipment rental, timeline, location-based pricing, PDF reports. Very close to our core.
- **Zamu Urban Living** — Kenyan cost calculator with **daily-updated material prices**
  and regional/skill-based labour rates. (Daily prices is a feature we *don't* have yet.)
- **PodCity / UjenziBora** — house cost estimator (construction, labour, materials,
  consultancy fees) aimed at "build from anywhere."
- **ConstructionKenya** building cost calculator; **Integrum** cost index.

**Implication:** the "cost calculator" niche in Kenya is already served by several
players — one with a live daily-price feed. We are *not* first here.

## 2. Global homeowner / AI BOQ tools — adjacent, fast-moving
- **Savvy Build** (UK) — AI estimator giving an itemised BoQ (materials/labour/plant)
  for homeowners. Closest global analogue to our homeowner BOQ idea.
- **Realx**, **Intextify**, **BoqCalc** — BOQ/takeoff tools; several now do **AI
  drawing → BOQ** (upload DWG/PDF, auto-extract quantities). This tech is advancing
  fast and could commoditise take-off.
- **Construction Cost Estimator** (Play) — simple material-quantity calculators (many exist).

## 3. Enterprise QS suites — upmarket, not our buyer
- **Cubit** (one-time **$2,995–4,995**), **CostX** (BIM takeoff), **Buildxact**
  (subscription, residential pros). Desktop/drawing-based, expensive, for QS/contractors.
  Confirms the reports' point: pro tools are out of reach for a self-builder. **But note
  Cubit is one-time-priced too** — "one-time fee" isn't unique to us.

## 4. Diaspora build-verification — our "Verified Build" tier is a CROWDED service market ⚠️⚠️
Several established Kenyan firms do exactly this as their *main business*:
- **Diaspora Build Safe** — independent oversight; visits sites, documents progress,
  **verifies work before payments are released** (near-identical to our Verified Build concept).
- **AlphaBridge Construction** — build-from-USA/UK/Canada; weekly video, daily photos,
  milestone reports, material verification, expense tracking.
- **Build With Stratum** — remote supervision, timestamped photos, private 24/7 dashboard.
- **House of Diaspora**, **Fingerprint Builders**, **Aalis Studios** — remote PM + oversight.

**Implication:** our Verified Build competes with dedicated operators who already have
boots on the ground. As a software add-on it's lighter than their full service — this is
a hard segment to win on features alone; partnership or referral may beat building.

---

## 5. Where Fatalibuilders is genuinely differentiated
Not the individual features — the **combination + rigour**:
1. **Design-code intelligence** (Eurocode / BS / KEBS, stamped on every doc) — the Kenyan
   calculators don't do this; it's our hardest-to-copy moat.
2. **Engineering-grade take-off from just dimensions** (not a rough per-m² number).
3. **The completeness wedge** — showing the % a quick quote hides — as the sales hook.
4. **One product, self-serve:** quantities + costed BOQ + labour + exports + M-Pesa +
   offline + 5 languages + $30 one-time. The Kenyan competitors are either *simpler
   calculators* or *service businesses*, not a rigorous self-serve product.

## 6. Real threats (be honest)
- **Kenyan calculators already exist** and one has **daily price feeds** we lack.
- **Diaspora verification is a competitive service market** with entrenched players.
- **AI drawing→BOQ** is improving fast and could erode the take-off advantage.
- Our rate data is a single 2026 card; competitors with live/regional prices look fresher.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Lead with the moat:** design-code rigour + completeness wedge + "engineering-grade,
   not a calculator." Don't compete as "another cost calculator."
2. **Close the daily-prices gap** (a roadmap daily-engagement item) — Zamu already has it.
3. **Reposition Verified Build** as *partner/referral* with an oversight firm rather than
   competing head-on, or narrow it to a "trust page + milestones" layer that complements
   their service.
4. **Move fast on distribution** — the category isn't empty, so mindshare (partnerships,
   hardware stores, QS/engineers, universities) is the race.
