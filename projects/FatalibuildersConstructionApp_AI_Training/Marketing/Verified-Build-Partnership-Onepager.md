## The opportunity

Kenyans abroad want to build back home but fear their money won't build what it
should. Two things solve that: **an accurate, trustworthy estimate up front**, and
**independent eyes on the site**. Fatalibuilders does the first at scale; you do
the second on the ground. Together we cover the whole journey — and neither of us
has to build the other half.

## What Fatalibuilders brings

- **A funnel of qualified, estimate-ready diaspora clients.** They enter their
  project, get an engineering-grade, design-code BOQ and budget, and land on a
  built-in **"Verified Build"** request — already knowing their numbers.
- **The software layer, free to you:** a shareable public **verification page**
  per project (live status, milestones foundation→handover, estimated contract
  value) that your field reports feed into — the client's single source of truth.
- **Payment rails** (M-Pesa + cards) and a multilingual, mobile-first product.
- **Brand + reach:** launch marketing across social, price transparency, SEO.

## What [Partner] brings

- Boots on the ground: **scheduled site visits, inspections, timestamped photos,
  material verification, milestone sign-off** in your coverage areas.
- Local supervision and the trust of a physical presence — the part software can't do.

## How it works

1. A diaspora client gets their estimate in the app and requests a **Verified Build**.
2. The request is **routed to [Partner]** with the project details and BOQ attached.
3. [Partner] schedules visits; each milestone check updates the **shared
   verification page** the client and their family watch from abroad.
4. Payment for oversight is collected in-app (or by you); **revenue is shared.**

## Commercial model (pick one to discuss)

- **Revenue share:** Fatalibuilders collects the Verified Build fee in-app and
  passes [Partner] an agreed % per project; or
- **Referral fee:** [Partner] prices its own service; Fatalibuilders earns a
  per-referral / per-conversion fee; or
- **Bundled tier:** a co-branded package (estimate + verification) at a set price,
  split on agreed terms.

## Why now

The diaspora-build market is active but **fragmented** — estimating tools and
oversight firms operate separately, leaving clients to stitch it together. A
combined, credible offer is a first-mover advantage for both of us.

## Next step

A 30-minute call to align on coverage areas and the commercial model, then a
small **pilot (3–5 projects)** to prove the flow before scaling.
